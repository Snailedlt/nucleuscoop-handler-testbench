Duo and Nucli UI kit

The mascots are the brand: Duo for splitscreen.me, Nucli for the handler tools. Every piece here carries one of them, or a bit of them (an electron, Duo's stripe, a player's colour), and moves the way they do: springs that overshoot a little, a squash on impact, a hold, then a snap. Messages appear where you acted, said by the mascot, not in a corner.

For the Nucleus Co-op handler tools, splitscreen.me, and anyone else who uses the mascots. A showcase: every piece is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and View code shows exactly what runs here. Use it as it is, or rebuild it in your own UI library; keep the mascots, the tokens and the timing notes. When someone turns motion or mascots off, every piece falls back to plain icons and text.

  • Find: the list at the side (Contents on a phone; / to search), or the overview below.
  • Try: every demo works. Switch the look, light or dark, and Motion at the top.
