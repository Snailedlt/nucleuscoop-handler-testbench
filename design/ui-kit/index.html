Duo and Nucli UI kit

A complete set of interface pieces that move the way the mascots do: springs that overshoot a little, a squash on impact, a hold, then a snap. It's a showcase: every piece is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and "View code" shows exactly what runs on this page. Use it as it is, or rebuild it in your own UI library; keep the tokens and the timing notes.

Try the looks, light and dark, and the Motion setting at the top. With "Still", or when your device asks for less motion, nothing moves but every state still shows; with "Off" (and in the alpha 8 look) the mascots step aside for plain icons.

    Duo is splitscreen.me's mascot, Nucli the handler tools'. Colours from the workbench's looks (playground/src/themes.json); player colours are the splitscreen.me button colours.
