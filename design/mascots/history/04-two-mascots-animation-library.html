Duo and Nucli

One mascot for splitscreen.me, one for the handler tools. Both grow out of the splitscreen.me logo: Duo is its split blue window, Nucli wears its gamepad's four button colours. One colour scheme each, the same in light and dark. The motion borrows BSManager's tricks: a wind-up before every move, squash and stretch, overshoot, and a pause before the spin.

Duo's animations

For the app, the Hub, the website and Discord.

Nucli's animations

For the workbench, the VS Code extension and the CLI.

In the product

Loaders and states, ready to drop in. Each is one small SVG with CSS; nothing moves when the device asks for less motion.

Discord emoji

Static and animated, at Discord's sizes: 22 px in a message, 48 px on their own, 16 px as reactions.

Small and in one colour

What I dropped, and why

Cushy (the couch) and Pip & Pod (the twins) were cute, but neither reads at 16 px, and the twins double every drawing. Nab (the bug-catcher) says "you made bugs", which is the wrong tone for beginners. Padd (the gamepad) is nearly a generic controller icon and repeats Duo's idea. The palette switcher is gone too: the logo's own colours work everywhere, so each mascot gets exactly one look.
