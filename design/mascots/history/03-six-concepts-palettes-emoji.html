Mascot concepts

Two mascots: one for all of splitscreen.me (Nucleus Co-op, the Hub, the website, Discord) and a sidekick for the handler tooling (the workbench, the VS Code extension, the CLI). Three ideas for each, with animations, Discord emoji, in four moods, at real sizes, on light and dark, in colours from splitscreen.me, v3.splitscreen.me or the Nucleus Co-op app (pick below). These are sketches to pitch, not final art.

Next steps

  1. Pick one of each (or mix: any project mascot works with any sidekick).
  2. Pitch it to the Nucleus Co-op maintainers on Discord. The project mascot is their call; the tooling one can start in the workbench.
  3. An artist (or a later pass from me) turns the chosen sketch into final art: a vector master, a one-colour version, emoji and a sticker sheet.
  4. In the workbench it goes into the empty Check tab, Test all, failed loads, the 404 page and the tips. Never in alpha 8, never moving when the device asks for less motion.
Original characters, drawn for this project; none of them is based on another app's mascot. Colours from the splitscreen.me logo, the v3 site and Hub, and Nucleus Co-op's default theme (theme.ini).
