Duo and Nucli

One mascot for splitscreen.me, one for the handler tools. Both grow out of the splitscreen.me logo: Duo is its split blue window; Nucli is a warm amber nucleus whose electrons wear the gamepad's four button colours. One colour scheme each, the same in light and dark. The motion borrows BSManager's tricks: a wind-up before every move, squash and stretch, overshoot, and a pause before the spin.

Players: split and merge

One mascot per player. Duo splits off a new friend when a player joins and two of them crash back into one when a player leaves; Nucli pops an electron out, or swallows one. Each player can be present (a controller Nucleus can see), assigned (placed on a screen, holding a controller) or active (their game is running). Try the buttons, or click a mascot.

1 player
  • Present Nucleus sees the controller. Duo looks around; Nucli's electron is a hollow ring.
  • Assigned Placed on a screen. Duo holds a controller, ready; the electron fills in.
  • Active Their game is running. Duo bounces and plays; the electron speeds up with a trail.

What I dropped, and why

Cushy (the couch) and Pip & Pod (the twins) were cute, but neither reads at 16 px, and the twins double every drawing. Nab (the bug-catcher) says "you made bugs", which is the wrong tone for beginners. Padd (the gamepad) is nearly a generic controller icon and repeats Duo's idea. The palette switcher is gone too: the logo's own colours work everywhere, so each mascot gets exactly one look.
